\section*{Scope and reading guide}
This supplement collects the full proofs, experimental protocols, and saved results.
Appendices A--E share their sources with the paper's integrated appendices. Appendix F
develops additional finite-population diagnostics from the experimental archive. The
accompanying ZIP supplies code, configurations, numerical outputs, and reproduction checks.

\paragraph{Revision experiments and checks.}
The finite-cell study separates oracle interval containment from learned-endpoint
accuracy. Controlled repayment masks check empirical enlargement and model comparisons
against known outcomes. The corrected sequential audit uses the retained plug-in
objective, with the historical outcome fit fixed and the predictor retrained after each
batch: 20 masks, six policies, and nine recorded rounds, with terminal comparisons paired
within mask. The code includes cohort-identity and optimization-equivalence checks.
This edition consolidates completed outputs; no new experiment was run.

\paragraph{How to interpret the evidence.}
Oracle coverage, learned-endpoint error, empirical fold enlargement, and sequential
retained width answer different questions. The additional diagnostics in Appendix F
use specified finite populations and, where stated, a finite cutoff grid. They do not
provide new finite-sample or adaptive confidence guarantees. The diabetes illustration
uses all known outcomes to define its strata and observation mechanism; it is not a
held-out clinical prediction evaluation.

\paragraph{Using the companion files.}
Keep this PDF beside \path{main.pdf} for links to the main text. Shared appendix and
bibliography numbering is preserved. The experiment guide maps saved results to commands
and separates current runs from archival procedures. Obtain raw borrower records
separately using the documented preparation instructions.
